# **SECTION 1: TOURNAMENT OVERVIEW**

## **1.1 Purpose**

**The Virtual Football Alliance (VFA) is an eFootball tournament organized to provide a competitive, fair, and professional environment where managers compete based on skill, strategy, sportsmanship, and respect.**

**This Official Rulebook establishes the regulations governing every aspect of the competition. All registered participants are expected to read, understand, and comply with these rules throughout the tournament.**

**Participation in any VFA competition automatically signifies acceptance of every rule contained within this document.**

---

## **1.2 Scope of the Rulebook**

**This Rulebook applies to every competition organized under the Virtual Football Alliance unless otherwise stated by the VFA Administration.**

**While certain tournament formats, team counts, schedules, or competition structures may vary between seasons, the general regulations contained within this Rulebook shall remain applicable unless officially amended.**

**Any season-specific information published by the VFA Administration shall be considered an official extension of this Rulebook.**

---

## **1.3 Objectives**

**The objectives of VFA are to:**

**1.3.1** **Promote fair and competitive gameplay.**  
**1.3.2** **Ensure equal opportunities for every participating club.**  
**1.3.3** **Maintain professionalism and sportsmanship throughout the tournament.**  
**1.3.4** **Provide transparent procedures for scheduling, disputes, penalties, and administration.**  
**1.3.5** **Create an organized competition that rewards skill, consistency, and integrity.**

---

## **1.4 Authority of the VFA Administration**

**The tournament shall be managed by the VFA Administration, consisting of four (4) official administrators appointed to organize and oversee all competitions.**

**The responsibilities of the VFA Administration include, but are not limited to:**

**1.4.1** **Organizing tournaments.**  
**1.4.2** **Publishing fixtures and schedules.**  
**1.4.3** **Registering teams and managers.**  
**1.4.4** **Managing league standings.**  
**1.4.5** **Resolving disputes.**  
**1.4.6** **Enforcing disciplinary actions.**  
**1.4.7** **Applying penalties.**  
**1.4.8** **Interpreting tournament regulations.**  
**1.4.9** **Updating official tournament information.**

**The VFA Administration shall always act in the best interest of maintaining fairness, consistency, and competitive integrity.**

---

## **1.5 Rule Amendments**

**The VFA Administration reserves the right to modify, clarify, update, or introduce additional rules whenever necessary.**

**Whenever possible, rule changes shall be officially announced before taking effect.**

**Rule amendments may become immediately effective if they are required to:**

**1.5.1** **Resolve unforeseen situations.**  
**1.5.2** **Protect competitive integrity.**  
**1.5.3** **Correct administrative errors.**  
**1.5.4** **Prevent abuse or exploitation of loopholes.**  
**1.5.5** **Improve tournament management.**

**All officially announced amendments shall be considered part of this Rulebook.**

---

## **1.6 Final Authority**

**Any situation not specifically covered by this Rulebook shall be reviewed by the VFA Administration.**

**The Administration shall interpret the rules according to the principles of fairness, consistency, and competitive integrity.**

**All official decisions made by the VFA Administration shall be final and binding upon every participant.**

# **SECTION 3: DEFINITIONS**

**Unless otherwise stated, the following definitions shall apply throughout this Rulebook.**

### **Administration**

**The official three-member governing body responsible for organizing, managing, and enforcing the tournament.**

---

### **Manager**

**The officially registered individual responsible for representing a club throughout the tournament.**

---

### **Active Manager**

**A manager who makes reasonable efforts to communicate, schedule, and complete fixtures before the official deadline.**

---

### **Club / Team**

**A registered participant competing in the tournament.**

**The terms Club and Team may be used interchangeably throughout this Rulebook.**

---

### **Fixture**

**A scheduled official match between two participating clubs.**

---

### **Matchday**

**A designated period during which specific fixtures must be completed.**

---

### **Home Team**

**The club officially designated as the home side for a fixture.**

**Home status may affect gameplay settings depending on tournament regulations.**

---

### **Away Team**

**The club officially designated as the away side for a fixture.**

---

### **Registered Player**

**A football player included in the officially approved squad for that club.**

---

### **Unregistered Player**

**Any player not included in the club's officially approved squad at the time of the match.**

---

### **Deadline**

**The final date and time determined by the VFA Administration for completing a required tournament activity.**

---

### **Screen Recording**

**A continuous video recording captured by a participating manager that satisfies all recording requirements established within this Rulebook.**

---

### **Official Announcement**

**Any information officially published by the VFA Administration through approved communication channels.**

**Official announcements carry the same authority as this Rulebook unless they directly contradict an existing regulation.**

---

### **Forfeit**

**A match result awarded by the Administration without the match being completed, usually due to rule violations, failure to appear, or disciplinary action.**

---

### **Decider Match**

**A final, tie-breaking fixture played to determine the winner of a knockout tie when both the aggregate score and away goals remain exactly equal after two legs.**

---

### **Transfer Window**

**A specific timeframe designated by the VFA Administration during which managers are permitted to officially acquire or release registered players.**

---

### **Smart Assist**

**An in-game eFootball feature that automates gameplay decisions. The use of Smart Assist is strictly prohibited in all official VFA fixtures.**

---

### **VPN (Virtual Private Network)**

**A third-party network routing application used by managers to stabilize connections during matchmaking when standard matchmaking fails.**

---

### **Match Fixing / Win Trading**

**The strictly prohibited act of deliberately manipulating the outcome or events of an official fixture, often through collusion between managers, to achieve a pre-determined result.**

# **SECTION 4: TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE**

## **4.1 General Structure**

**The VFA season may consist of one or more official competitions.**

**The format, number of participating teams, qualification criteria, and tournament structure may vary from season to season.**

**All season-specific formats shall be announced before the tournament begins.**

**For Season 8, the competitions are:**

**4.1.1** **VFA Premier League (VPL)**  
**4.1.2** **VFA Champions League (VCL)**

---

# **4.2 VFA Premier League (VPL)**

**The VFA Premier League is the primary league competition of the season.**

**For Season 8:**

**4.2.1** **Participating Clubs: 12**  
**4.2.2** **Competition Format: Double Round-Robin**  
**4.2.3** **Each club plays every other club twice.**  
**4.2.4** **One fixture shall be played as the Home Team.**  
**4.2.5** **One fixture shall be played as the Away Team.**

**Therefore:**

**4.2.6** **Each club plays a total of twenty-two (22) league matches, consisting of eleven (11) home fixtures and eleven (11) away fixtures.**

**With 12 clubs:**

**4.2.7** **11 Home Matches**  
**4.2.8** **11 Away Matches**  
**4.2.9** **Total \= 22 League Matches**

---

### **League Points**

**Win \= 3 Points**

**Draw \= 1 Point**

**Loss \= 0 Points**

---

### **League Ranking**

**Clubs shall be ranked according to:**

1. **Total Points**  
2. **Goal Difference**  
3. **Goals Scored**  
4. **Head-to-Head Record**  
5. **Fair Play Record (See Section 15.8)**

**If two or more clubs remain identical after applying every tiebreaker, the VFA Administration shall determine the final ranking using an officially announced procedure.**

---

### **League Champion**

**The club finishing first in the final league standings shall be declared the VFA Premier League Champion.**

---

# **4.3 VFA Champions League (VCL)**

**The VFA Champions League uses a Swiss League Stage followed by a Knockout Stage.**

**The Knockout Stage shall consist of a Playoff Round, Quarter-Finals, Semi-Finals, and Final.**

**For Season 8:**

**4.3.1** **Participating Clubs: 12**

---

## **4.4 Pot Allocation**

**Before the Swiss Stage Draw, all participating clubs shall be divided into competitive pots.**

**Pot allocation shall primarily be determined using previous tournament performances, rankings, or other criteria announced by the VFA Administration.**

**The purpose of pot allocation is to create a balanced schedule while maintaining competitive fairness.**

---

## **4.5 Swiss League Draw**

**The Swiss League fixtures shall be generated using the official VFA Swiss Draw Algorithm developed by the VFA Administration.**

**The algorithm shall ensure that every participating club satisfies all tournament requirements simultaneously.**

---

### **League Stage Requirements**

**Each club shall play exactly six (6) league matches.**

**Each club shall face exactly six unique opponents.**

**Every club shall face:**

**4.5.1** **Two opponents from Pot 1**  
**4.5.2** **Two opponents from Pot 2**  
**4.5.3** **Two opponents from Pot 3**

**Teams are permitted to play opponents from their own pot provided all draw restrictions remain satisfied.**

---

### **Home and Away Balance**

**Every club shall play:**

**4.5.4** **Three Home matches.**  
**4.5.5** **Three Away matches.**

**Additionally:**

**4.5.6** **One Home match against a Pot 1 club.**  
**4.5.7** **One Away match against a Pot 1 club.**  
**4.5.8** **One Home match against a Pot 2 club.**  
**4.5.9** **One Away match against a Pot 2 club.**  
**4.5.10** **One Home match against a Pot 3 club.**  
**4.5.11** **One Away match against a Pot 3 club.**

**This guarantees a balanced schedule for every participant.**

---

### **Match Restrictions**

**The Swiss Draw Algorithm shall enforce the following restrictions:**

**4.5.12** **A club may not face itself.**  
**4.5.13** **A club may not face the same opponent more than once.**  
**4.5.14** **Every opponent must be unique.**  
**4.5.15** **Every scheduling requirement must be satisfied before the draw is finalized.**

**Example:**

**✅ Astral Warriors vs Beast Era FC**

**❌ Astral Warriors vs Beast Era FC twice.**

**❌ Astral Warriors vs Astral Warriors.**

---

## **4.6 Knockout Qualification**

**Following completion of the Swiss Stage standings:**

**4.6.1** **1st and 2nd qualify directly for the Semi-finals.**  
**4.6.2** **3rd and 4th qualify directly for the Quarter-finals.**  
**4.6.3** **5th to 8th qualify for the Playoff Round.**  
**4.6.4** **9th to 12th are eliminated from the competition.**

### **Swiss Stage Tiebreakers**

**If two or more clubs are tied on points at the end of the Swiss Stage, the same tiebreaker criteria from the VFA Premier League shall be applied:**

1. **Total Points**
2. **Goal Difference**
3. **Goals Scored**
4. **Head-to-Head Record (Ignored if the tied teams did not play against each other during the Swiss Stage)**
5. **Fair Play Record (See Section 15.8)**

**The knockout bracket shall follow the official tournament structure published by the VFA Administration before the knockout stage begins.**

# **SECTION 5: MATCH SETTINGS**

## **5.1 Official Game Platform**

**All official VFA matches shall be played exclusively using eFootball™ 2026 Mobile, including both Android and iOS devices.**

**Participants may use any available game server provided both managers are able to successfully establish a stable match connection.**

---

## **5.2 Eligible Squads**

**Each manager may only use the squad officially registered under their club.**

**Players may only be added to or removed from a squad through official VFA procedures, including auctions and approved transfer windows.**

**The strength, rarity, or card type of an eFootball player (including Epic, Big Time, Show Time, Featured, Highlight, Standard, or any future card type) shall not affect eligibility.**

**The only determining factor is whether the player is officially registered to that club.**

**Example**

**✅ Wretchedly Terrible FC has officially acquired Mbappé through the VFA Transfer Window. The manager may use any eligible Mbappé card available in their account.**

**❌ Astral Warriors uses a player who is officially registered to Beast Era FC. This constitutes the use of an unregistered player and shall be subject to the penalties described in Section 11.7.**

---

## **5.3 Match Duration**

**Unless otherwise announced, every official VFA match shall use:**

**5.3.1** **Match Duration: 8 Minutes**  
**5.3.2** **Maximum Substitutions: 5**

**Should a future version of eFootball remove or modify these settings, the VFA Administration shall determine the closest suitable alternative.**

---

## **5.4 Player Condition Settings**

**The designated player condition shall be configured before every official match.**

### **League Stage Matches**

**5.4.1** **Home Team: Excellent**  
**5.4.2** **Away Team: Good**

### **Knockout Stage Matches**

**5.4.3** **Home Team: Excellent**  
**5.4.4** **Away Team: Good**

### **Final and any Decider Match**

**Both participating teams shall use:**

**5.4.5** **Excellent Condition**

**Managers are responsible for ensuring the correct player condition is selected before kickoff.**

---

## **5.5 Formations and Tactics**

**Managers have complete freedom regarding tactical decisions.**

**There shall be no restrictions on:**

**5.5.1** **Formation**  
**5.5.2** **Team Playstyle**  
**5.5.3** **Offensive Instructions**  
**5.5.4** **Defensive Instructions**  
**5.5.5** **Pressing Style**  
**5.5.6** **Attacking Style**  
**5.5.7** **Player Roles**  
**5.5.8** **Any other tactical setting available within the game.**

**Success shall be determined solely through legitimate gameplay.**

---

## **5.6 Match Settings Responsibility**

**Before the match begins, both managers are responsible for confirming that:**

**5.6.1** **The correct squads are being used.**  
**5.6.2** **The required player condition has been selected.**  
**5.6.3** **Both players have a stable internet connection.**

**Failure to verify these settings before kickoff shall not automatically justify a replay unless specifically permitted under this Rulebook.**

---

# **SECTION 6: MATCH PROCEDURE**

## **6.1 Match Organization**

**For every official fixture, the Home Team shall have the initial responsibility for organizing the match.**

**The Home Manager shall decide:**

**6.1.1** **Who creates the match room.**  
**6.1.2** **Whether a VPN is required for matchmaking.**  
**6.1.3** **Which manager connects to the VPN when necessary.**

**If matchmaking is successful without a VPN, no VPN shall be used.**

**The Away Manager may not insist upon using a VPN unless both managers mutually agree.**

---

## **6.2 Match Room Creation**

**Normally, the Home Manager shall create the match room.**

**If the Home Manager is unable to create the room due to technical or connectivity issues, the Away Manager may create the room instead.**

**Changing the room creator shall not affect Home or Away status for tournament purposes.**

---

## **6.3 Pre-Match Responsibilities**

**Before kickoff, both managers should verify:**

**6.3.1** **Correct squads.**  
**6.3.2** **Correct player condition.**  
**6.3.3** **Stable internet connection.**

**Managers are encouraged to resolve any incorrect settings before the match begins.**

---

## **6.4 Smart Assist Verification**

**Each manager is solely responsible for ensuring that Smart Assist is disabled before entering the match.**

**Managers are not required to verify the Smart Assist setting of their opponent before kickoff.**

**Compliance shall instead be verified through the mandatory recording submitted after the match.**

---

## **6.5 Incorrect Match Settings**

**Managers are expected to verify correct settings (such as condition and duration) before entering the match.**

**If an incorrect match duration or incorrect player condition is noticed before the match starts, the match must be restarted using the proper settings.**

**If a manager fails to notice incorrect settings before entering the room, but realizes the condition arrows are incorrect once inside the pre-match Game Plan screen, they are permitted to immediately quit the match from the Game Plan screen without penalty. The manager must capture screenshot proof of the incorrect condition arrows to justify the quit.**

**Managers who proceed to play the match with clearly incorrect settings may lose the right to request a replay unless the VFA Administration determines otherwise.**

---

## **6.6 Incorrect Squad Usage**

**If a manager believes the opponent used an illegal or unregistered player, a complaint may be submitted within the official complaint period.**

**The VFA Administration shall investigate the matter and apply penalties where appropriate.**

---

## **6.7 Failure to Record**

**A match may still be played even if a manager forgets to begin recording.**

**However, failure to submit a valid recording after the match shall be treated as a violation of the Smart Assist policy and handled according to the penalties described in Section 8.6.**

---

# **SECTION 7: KNOCKOUT STAGE**

## **7.1 General Format**

**The Knockout Stage shall consist of:**

**7.1.1** **Playoff Round**  
**5th vs 8th**  
**6th vs 7th**

**7.1.2** **Quarter-Finals**  
**3rd vs Winner of 5th/8th**  
**4th vs Winner of 6th/7th**

**7.1.3** **Semi-Finals**  
**1st vs Winner of QF2**  
**2nd vs Winner of QF1**

**7.1.4** **Final**

**Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2**

**Except for the Final, every knockout tie shall be played over two legs.**

---

## **7.2 Aggregate Score**

**The winner of each knockout tie shall be determined using the aggregate score from both legs.**

**The goals scored by each club across both matches shall be added together.**

**The club with the higher aggregate score shall advance.**

---

## **7.3 Away Goals Rule**

**If the aggregate score is level after both legs, the Away Goals Rule shall be applied.**

**The club that has scored more goals while playing as the Away Team shall advance.**

**Example**

**First Leg**

**Astral Warriors 2-1 Beast Era FC**

**Second Leg**

**Beast Era FC 1-0 Astral Warriors**

**Aggregate:**

**2-2**

**Away Goals:**

**Astral Warriors: 1**

**Beast Era FC: 1**

**Since both clubs have scored an equal number of away goals, a Decider Match shall be played.**

---

## **7.4 Decider Match**

**When both aggregate score and away goals remain equal, a Decider Match shall determine the winner.**

**The Decider Match shall use:**

**7.4.1** **Match Duration: 6 Minutes**  
**7.4.2** **Both Teams: Excellent Condition**  
**7.4.3** **Extra Time: Enabled**  
**7.4.4** **Penalty Shootout: Enabled**

**The Home Team from the Second Leg shall retain Home status for the Decider Match.**

*(Note: This simulates real-life football where Extra Time and Penalties take place at the stadium of the Second Leg. Retaining Home status acts as a justified reward for the team that performed better in previous rounds and earned the 2nd leg home advantage.)*

**Accordingly, that manager shall determine:**

**7.4.5** **Match room creation.**  
**7.4.6** **VPN requirement.**  
**7.4.7** **VPN assignment when necessary.**

---

## **7.5 Winner**

**The winner of the Decider Match shall advance to the next round.**

**No replay shall be granted solely because a match proceeds into Extra Time or Penalty Shootouts.**

# **SECTION 8: SMART ASSIST & SCREEN RECORDING**

## **8.1 Smart Assist Policy**

**Smart Assist must remain OFF throughout every official VFA match.**

**Enabling Smart Assist at any point during an official match constitutes a violation of tournament regulations.**

---

## **8.2 Mandatory Recording**

**Every participating manager must submit one continuous, unedited screen recording following each official match.**

**The recording serves as official verification of tournament compliance.**

---

## **8.3 Recording Requirements**

**The recording must clearly display:**

**8.3.1** **A portion of the completed match.**  
**8.3.2** **The Smart Assist confirmation screen showing Smart Assist is OFF.**  
**8.3.3** **The device notification bar displaying the recording time.**

**The recording does not need to include the entire match.**

**Managers may begin recording at any point provided every required element appears within one continuous recording.**

---

## **8.4 Editing Restrictions**

**The submitted recording must remain completely unedited.**

**The following are prohibited:**

**8.4.1** **Cutting**  
**8.4.2** **Trimming**  
**8.4.3** **Joining multiple clips**  
**8.4.4** **Speed adjustments**  
**8.4.5** **Visual editing**  
**8.4.6** **Any modification that interrupts recording continuity**

**Only one continuous recording shall be accepted.**

---

## **8.5 Submission Deadline**

**Every manager must submit the required recording within 15 minutes after completion of the match.**

**Recordings shall be submitted through the Official VFA Messenger Notice Group unless the VFA Administration announces an alternative submission method.**

---

## **8.6 Failure to Submit**

**Managers who fail to submit a valid recording within the required time may be subject to disciplinary action.**

**Possible consequences include:**

**8.6.1** **Match review.**  
**8.6.2** **Point deduction.**  
**8.6.3** **Match forfeit.**  
**8.6.4** **Additional penalties for repeated violations.**

**The VFA Administration shall determine the appropriate sanction based upon the circumstances.**

---

## **8.7 Recording Responsibility**

**Managers are solely responsible for ensuring that their recording is properly captured and preserved until submission.**

**Accidental recording failure, corrupted files, device malfunction, or similar technical issues shall remain the responsibility of the manager and shall not exempt them from tournament regulations.**

# **SECTION 9: TECHNICAL ISSUES & CONNECTIVITY**

## **9.1 General Principle**

**Unexpected technical problems may occur during official matches.**

**Whenever possible, matches shall continue from the point at which play was interrupted rather than being replayed in full.**

---

## **9.2 Disconnect Procedure**

**If either manager experiences:**

**9.2.1** **Network disconnection.**  
**9.2.2** **Connection instability.**  
**9.2.3** **Unexpected game closure.**  
**9.2.4** **Device failure.**  
**9.2.5** **Other legitimate technical issues,**

**the interrupted manager shall immediately notify the opponent.**

**Either manager may report the incident.**

---

## **9.3 Match Replay Rule**

**When an official match is interrupted, a new room shall be created and a full standard match will be started.**

**The replayed match shall continue for:**

**Remaining Match Time \+ 3 Minutes**

**The additional three minutes are intended to compensate for stoppage during reconnection.**

**IMPORTANT: Only goals scored *before* this calculated remaining time mark will count towards the official result. Once the in-game clock reaches this minute mark, managers may either mutually abandon the match or choose to play it out. Any goals scored after the designated minute mark will be entirely ignored.**

**Example**

**Original Match:**

**Disconnect at 49'**

**Score:**

**Astral Warriors 2-1 Beast Era FC**

**Remaining Time:**

**41 Minutes**

**Replay Match Target Time:**

**44 Minutes (Only goals scored before the 44th minute count)**

**If the replay finishes (at or before 44'):**

**Astral Warriors 1-0 Beast Era FC**

**Official Final Result:**

**Astral Warriors 3-1 Beast Era FC**

---

## **9.4 Multiple Disconnects**

**If another disconnection occurs during the replay, the same procedure shall continue.**

**However, the additional 3 compensation minutes shall only be awarded once, during the first replay.**

**Example:**

**Original Match**

**Disconnect at 49'**

**Replay:**

**44 Minutes**

**Disconnect again after 20 replay minutes**

**Remaining Time:**

**24 Minutes**

**Final Replay:**

**24 Minutes**

**No additional compensation time shall be added.**

**This process shall continue until the fixture is successfully completed.**

---

## **9.5 Game Freeze or Technical Malfunction**

**If a manager experiences:**

**9.5.1** **Game freeze.**  
**9.5.2** **Severe gameplay malfunction.**  
**9.5.3** **Device crash.**

**the manager may manually close the application or disconnect in order to preserve match integrity.**

**Evidence, such as screenshots or video recordings, should be retained whenever possible.**

**The standard Disconnect Procedure shall then apply.**

---

## **9.6 Intentional Abuse**

**Any manager found deliberately abusing the Disconnect Procedure in order to gain an unfair sporting advantage shall be subject to disciplinary action.**

**The VFA Administration shall determine whether the evidence supports intentional misconduct.**

---

## **9.7 VPN Policy**

**The Home Manager may determine whether a VPN is required for matchmaking.**

**If matchmaking succeeds without a VPN, neither manager shall be required to use one.**

**When a VPN is required, the Home Manager shall decide which manager connects to the VPN.**

**The Home Manager may not require unnecessary VPN usage once normal matchmaking is available.**

---

# **SECTION 10: MATCH SCHEDULING & DEADLINES**

## **10.1 Official Match Schedule**

**The official fixture list for every competition shall be prepared and published by the VFA Administration.**

**The published fixture schedule shall include the matches to be played and the deadline for their completion.**

**Managers are not responsible for creating or modifying the official fixture schedule.**

**Each participating manager is solely responsible for ensuring that every assigned fixture is completed before the published deadline.**

---

## **10.2 Manager Communication**

**After the official schedule has been published, both participating managers are expected to communicate in order to arrange a suitable time to play their match.**

**Communication should be conducted through the Official VFA Messenger Group whenever possible.**

**Using the official group allows scheduling discussions to remain visible to both the VFA Administration and other participants, making dispute resolution easier if required.**

**Managers may also communicate through private messages.**

**However, private conversations are not recommended because they cannot be independently verified unless screenshots or other evidence are later provided.**

**The VFA Administration may request scheduling evidence at any time when investigating a scheduling dispute.**

---

## **10.3 Weekly Deadline**

**Every official fixture shall have a completion deadline announced by the VFA Administration.**

**All scheduled matches must be completed before this deadline unless an official extension has been granted.**

**Managers are encouraged to complete fixtures as early as possible rather than waiting until the final day.**

---

## **10.4 Deadline Extensions**

**The VFA Administration may extend one or more deadlines whenever doing so is considered necessary for the fair operation of the tournament.**

**Extensions may be granted due to, but are not limited to:**

**10.4.1** **eFootball server maintenance.**  
**10.4.2** **Server instability or widespread connection issues.**  
**10.4.3** **National holidays.**  
**10.4.4** **Natural disasters or emergencies.**  
**10.4.5** **Unavoidable personal circumstances presented by a manager.**  
**10.4.6** **Any other situation considered appropriate by the VFA Administration.**

**Granting a deadline extension remains entirely at the discretion of the VFA Administration.**

---

## **10.5 Playing After the Deadline**

**Once the official deadline has passed, managers shall not play the fixture unless explicit approval has first been granted by the VFA Administration.**

**Even if both managers mutually agree to play after the deadline, the match shall not be considered official without prior administrative approval.**

**Matches played after the deadline without authorization may be declared void.**

---

## **10.6 Inactive Managers**

**Managers are expected to remain reasonably active throughout the tournament.**

**A manager who becomes inactive without prior notice and fails to complete a scheduled fixture before the deadline may receive a Forfeit Loss for that fixture.**

**If the inactive manager later returns and provides a genuine explanation supported by sufficient evidence, the VFA Administration may allow the fixture to be played or may uphold the original forfeit result.**

**The decision shall depend upon the specific circumstances of each case.**

**The active manager must also demonstrate reasonable efforts to schedule the match by remaining active in the official communication group.**

**Failure to make reasonable scheduling efforts may affect the Administration's decision.**

---

## **10.7 Final Day Scheduling**

**When a fixture is intended to be played on the final day before the deadline, both managers must publicly agree upon an exact match time before the end of the previous day.**

**Only specific times shall be recognized as valid scheduling confirmations.**

**Examples of valid scheduling:**

**10.7.1** **Tomorrow at 8:00 PM**  
**10.7.2** **Deadline Day at 10:30 PM**  
**10.7.3** **Saturday at 9:15 PM**

**Examples of invalid scheduling:**

**10.7.4** **Tonight**  
**10.7.5** **Later**  
**10.7.6** **After midnight**  
**10.7.7** **I'll let you know**  
**10.7.8** **Whenever you're free**

**Vague scheduling statements shall not be considered valid.**

---

## **10.8 Failure to Appear**

**Once an official match time has been publicly confirmed, both managers are expected to appear on time.**

**A manager shall wait exactly ten (10) minutes after the agreed match time before requesting administrative action.**

**Example:**

**Scheduled Match Time:**

**9:00 PM**

**Opponent arrives:**

**9:08 PM ✅ On Time**

**9:10 PM ✅ Final Acceptable Time**

**9:11 PM ❌ Late**

**The waiting manager may voluntarily choose to continue with the fixture after ten minutes provided the original tournament deadline has not yet expired.**

**Otherwise, the manager may request administrative review.**

**The VFA Administration shall determine whether the absent manager had a valid reason.**

---

## **10.9 Automatic Results**

**Unless otherwise decided by the VFA Administration:**

**10.9.1** **If one manager fails to complete the fixture before the deadline, that manager shall receive a 3-0 Forfeit Loss, while the opponent shall receive a 3-0 Forfeit Win.**  
**10.9.2** **If both managers fail to complete the fixture before the deadline, the fixture shall normally be recorded as a 0-0 Forfeit Draw.**

**The VFA Administration reserves the right to make alternative decisions where exceptional circumstances exist.**

---

# **SECTION 11: SQUAD REGISTRATION & TRANSFER RULES**

## **11.1 Registered Squad**

**Every participating club must maintain an officially registered squad throughout the tournament.**

**A squad must contain at least twenty-four (24) players.**

**There is no maximum squad size, unless otherwise announced by the VFA Administration.**

---

## **11.2 Squad Eligibility**

**Managers may only use football players who are officially registered to their club.**

**Ownership of a player inside the eFootball game does not automatically make that player eligible for VFA competition.**

**Only players appearing on the club's officially approved squad list may participate.**

---

## **11.3 Transfer Windows**

**The VFA Administration shall determine the dates of all transfer windows.**

**Normally, each season shall contain:**

**11.3.1** **One Transfer Window before the tournament begins.**  
**11.3.2** **One Mid-Season Transfer Window.**

**The Administration may modify this structure whenever necessary.**

---

## **11.4 Emergency Transfers**

**Emergency transfers outside an official transfer window are generally prohibited.**

**However, the VFA Administration may approve an emergency transfer in exceptional situations where it believes such approval is necessary for the fair continuation of the tournament.**

**Approval of emergency transfers shall remain entirely at the discretion of the Administration.**

---

## **11.5 Player Eligibility After Transfer**

**Players transferred during an official transfer window shall become eligible from the next Matchday after the transfer window has officially closed, unless the VFA Administration announces otherwise.**

**Managers shall not use newly transferred players before they become officially eligible.**

---

## **11.6 Administrative Delay**

**If the VFA Administration has officially approved a transfer but the website or squad database has not yet been updated due to administrative delay, the transferred player shall still be considered legally registered.**

**Managers shall not be penalized for delays caused solely by administrative processing.**

---

## **11.7 Unregistered Players**

**Using an unregistered player constitutes a serious rule violation.**

**Penalties are:**

### **VFA Premier League**

**Use of an unregistered player shall normally result in a one (1) point deduction.**

### **VFA Champions League**

**Use of an unregistered player shall normally result in a Forfeit Loss.**

**The VFA Administration may impose additional disciplinary action if circumstances warrant.**

---

## **11.8 Multiple Violations**

**If both participating clubs are found to have used unregistered players in the same fixture, both clubs shall receive the applicable penalties.**

**The VFA Administration shall determine any additional disciplinary measures after reviewing the specific circumstances.**

---

# **SECTION 12: MATCH RESULT SUBMISSION**

## **12.1 Submission Responsibility**

**Both participating managers are responsible for submitting the official match result.**

**Submitting results promptly helps ensure accurate tournament records and timely resolution of any discrepancies.**

---

## **12.2 Submission Deadline**

**Official match submissions must be completed within fifteen (15) minutes after the match concludes.**

**Late submissions may be reviewed by the VFA Administration.**

**Repeated late submissions may result in disciplinary action.**

---

## **12.3 Required Match Information**

**Every result submission must include:**

**12.3.1** **Final Score Screenshot.**  
**12.3.2** **Player Ratings.**  
**12.3.3** **Goal Scorers.**  
**12.3.4** **Assist Providers.**

**Only clear and readable submissions shall be accepted.**

---

## **12.4 Statistical Accuracy**

**Managers are responsible for ensuring that all submitted information is accurate.**

**Submitting incorrect statistics, whether intentional or accidental, may require administrative review.**

---

## **12.5 Conflicting Submissions**

**If the two managers submit conflicting information regarding:**

**12.5.1** **Final score.**  
**12.5.2** **Goal scorers.**  
**12.5.3** **Assists.**  
**12.5.4** **Player ratings.**

**The VFA Administration shall review the available evidence and determine the official statistics.**

**The Administration's decision shall become the official tournament record.**

---

## **12.6 Statistical Corrections**

**Once the VFA Administration has finalized and published official match statistics, no further corrections shall normally be permitted.**

**Exceptions may only be made where clear administrative error is identified by the VFA Administration.**

# **SECTION 13: COMPLAINTS & DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

## **13.1 Right to Submit a Complaint**

**Every participating manager has the right to submit an official complaint if they believe that:**

**13.1.1** **A tournament rule has been violated.**  
**13.1.2** **An opponent has gained an unfair advantage.**  
**13.1.3** **Incorrect match information has been submitted.**  
**13.1.4** **Tournament regulations have not been properly followed.**  
**13.1.5** **Any other issue has affected the integrity of a fixture.**

**Complaints shall be reviewed fairly and impartially by the VFA Administration.**

---

## **13.2 Complaint Deadline**

**All complaints relating to an official fixture must be submitted within thirty (30) minutes after the completion of that match.**

**Complaints submitted after this period may be rejected unless the VFA Administration determines that exceptional circumstances prevented timely submission.**

---

## **13.3 Acceptable Evidence**

**Managers are responsible for providing sufficient evidence to support their claims.**

**Acceptable evidence may include, but is not limited to:**

**13.3.1** **Screenshots**  
**13.3.2** **Screen recordings**  
**13.3.3** **Match recordings**  
**13.3.4** **Chat conversations**  
**13.3.5** **Official tournament messages**  
**13.3.6** **System-generated information**  
**13.3.7** **Any other material capable of proving the reported incident**

**The VFA Administration may request additional evidence whenever necessary.**

**Failure to provide sufficient evidence may result in the complaint being dismissed.**

---

## **13.4 Spectator Reports**

**Individuals who are not directly participating in a fixture may report serious rule violations if they possess credible evidence.**

**Examples include:**

**13.4.1** **Cheating**  
**13.4.2** **Account sharing**  
**13.4.3** **Match fixing**  
**13.4.4** **Fake evidence**  
**13.4.5** **Other serious violations affecting tournament integrity**

**The VFA Administration shall determine whether such reports require formal investigation.**

---

## **13.5 Investigation Procedure**

**Upon receiving a complaint, the VFA Administration may:**

**13.5.1** **Review submitted evidence.**  
**13.5.2** **Request additional evidence from either manager.**  
**13.5.3** **Interview the involved managers.**  
**13.5.4** **Review official tournament records.**  
**13.5.5** **Examine match recordings.**  
**13.5.6** **Consider any other relevant information.**

**Every investigation shall be conducted fairly and confidentially.**

---

## **13.6 Administrative Decision**

**After reviewing all available evidence, the VFA Administration shall issue an official decision.**

**The decision may include:**

**13.6.1** **Dismissal of the complaint.**  
**13.6.2** **Warning.**  
**13.6.3** **Match replay.**  
**13.6.4** **Match forfeit.**  
**13.6.5** **Point deduction.**  
**13.6.6** **Suspension.**  
**13.6.7** **Any other disciplinary action considered appropriate.**

**Every decision shall prioritize:**

**13.6.8** **Fair play.**  
**13.6.9** **Competitive integrity.**  
**13.6.10** **Consistency.**  
**13.6.11** **Tournament regulations.**

---

## **13.7 Appeals**

**Managers may request a review of an administrative decision if they believe:**

**13.7.1** **Important evidence was overlooked.**  
**13.7.2** **New evidence has become available.**  
**13.7.3** **A significant administrative error occurred.**

**Appeals shall be reviewed internally by the VFA Administration.**

**Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that the original decision will be changed.**

---

## **13.8 Final Decision**

**After completion of the appeal review, the decision announced by the VFA Administration shall be considered final and binding.**

**No further appeals shall be accepted for the same matter unless the Administration independently decides otherwise.**

**15.6.4** **Previous behaviour.**
